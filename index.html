RSK DESIGN STUDIO

RSK Design Studio is the identity I created at the start of my journey in 3D modeling, graphic design and visual development. This Instagram page showcases the results of my work as I explore how ideas can be transformed into clear and refined visuals. It began as a student’s attempt to practice design, but it has grown into a space where I present polished outcomes that reflect my evolving skills. The page is not restricted to civil engineering. It captures the wider range of creative interests that shape how I think: architectural forms, spatial concepts, graphic compositions and digital models. Each post represents a completed idea, whether it comes from technical curiosity or pure creative exploration.
